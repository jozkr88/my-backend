# Causal Model Governance and Evidence

Causal intelligence needs stronger evidence discipline than ordinary semantic
retrieval. A relationship can be a hypothesis, a discovered association, a
supported cause, a refuted cause, or a context-dependent cause. The status must
be visible to the runtime and traceable to data, assumptions, estimators,
refutation results, and model versions.

The model context should carry the result, uncertainty, assumptions, and
provenance—not only a natural-language conclusion. If the evidence is
insufficient, Joz should answer at the association level or ask for the missing
dataset and assumptions.
