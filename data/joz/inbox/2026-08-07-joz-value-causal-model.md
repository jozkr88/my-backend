# Joz Value Pathways

This additive causal-intelligence dataset explains Joz's business value as
evidence-bounded pathways rather than unsupported financial causation:

```text
Capability or skill
  → method applied
  → evidence or proof
  → plausible business outcome
  → context and measurement required
```

The principal pathways are:

- Agentic AI architecture → governed orchestration and context engineering → trusted decision support and usable execution.
- Systems and decision intelligence → dependency, ownership, feedback-loop, and constraint mapping → better prioritisation and execution.
- Human-centred and spatial AI → multimodal and spatial interaction → stronger adoption and decision confidence.
- Causal decision architecture → explicit assumptions, evidence, interventions, and model versions → safer movement from observation to action.

These are structured value pathways, not measured causal effects. Personal
production proof, framework guidance, hypotheses, and future business outcomes
must remain distinguishable. A real ROI or intervention claim requires an
organisation-specific baseline, treatment, outcome, time window, assumptions,
and validation data.
